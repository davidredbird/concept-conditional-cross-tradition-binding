# Concept-Conditional Cross-Tradition Binding

[![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.20261823.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20261823)

A research project at the intersection of contemplative traditions, philosophy of mind, and machine learning.

## The core question

Do the world's contemplative and mystical traditions, across unconnected cultures and millennia, converge on a shared structural description of reality? If yes, what is that structure, and what does it predict?

## The approach

Treat the world's mystical/contemplative literature as a multi-millennium dataset of hypotheses about reality, generated by serious inquirers using introspective methods. Test the convergence claim rigorously with modern NLP. Strip cultural and religious vocabulary off any real structural agreement and produce neutral feature definitions that science can engage with directly.

## Current results (Phase 0 → Phase 2)

### Phase 0 — paraphrase-heavy curated corpus (143 passages, 23 traditions, 5 analyses)

Replicated across two unrelated embedding models (OpenAI `text-embedding-3-large` 3072-dim proprietary and open-source `MiniLM-L6-v2` 384-dim via ONNX Runtime):

- **H1 (cross-tradition convergence of historical contemplative nondual traditions): supported.** *p* < 0.0001 in every run. The Stace–Forman perennialist claim, debated qualitatively for 65+ years, is empirically defensible at this corpus size.
- **Three-cluster structure** in the unsubstituted data: historical contemplative nondual / modern computational nondual (simulation theory, information physics, mathematical universe, analytic idealism, interface theory) / dualistic + non-contemplative controls.
- **Concept-level binding** is statistically significant for **5 of 7** pre-registered structural concepts:
  - **AWARENESS** binding: +0.11 (OpenAI) to +0.20 (BERT) — strongest signal, *p* < 0.0001
  - **RECOGNITION** (liberation/awakening): the classical perennialist signal across Advaita, Dzogchen, Sufism, Neoplatonism
  - **ULTIMATE**, **WORLD**, **SUBSTRATE**: all *p* ≤ 0.01
- **Mahayana × Theravada at 0.518 on AWARENESS** is the canonical cross-tradition concept-binding finding (Buddhist intra-tradition, nondual × dualistic, neither side wrote toward the comparison; replaces Rovelli–Nagarjuna as the cleaner poster-child result).

### Phase 1a — whole-book verified-translation replication (20 books, 920 chunks, ~2.85M tokens, 0% paraphrase)

The Phase 0 paper Draft 2 noted that **only 1 of 5 binding concepts (ULTIMATE)** could be evaluated on the non-paraphrase Phase 0 subset. Phase 1a converts that to:

- **All 5 binding concepts survive at *p* ≤ 0.0015** on verified published whole-book translations — **above the second-reviewer prior** of ~60% AWARENESS+RECOGNITION / ~40% full pattern.
- **SUBSTRATE binding is essentially unchanged** between phases (+0.0541 Phase 1a vs +0.0526 Phase 0).
- AWARENESS, RECOGNITION, WORLD, ULTIMATE deflate 3–4× at *passage* granularity but only 25–30% at *sentence* granularity. The differential deflation is mechanistically explained by **vocabulary-breadth-as-noise-floor**: pattern dictionaries containing common English terms (`consciousness`, `God`, `world`) fire on passages that mention the term casually rather than engage the concept technically, diluting passage-level binding; SUBSTRATE's technical-only dictionary (`emptiness`, `śūnyatā`, `implicate order`, `holographic`) has no such noise floor.

### Phase 1b — multi-translator within-source variance test (Draft 6)

Phase 1b addresses §9 limitation 1 (translator-as-confound) with multi-translator coverage of two source families (Bhagavad Gita and Tao Te Ching, three translators each). Externally pre-registered prior to running the analysis (commit-timestamped pre-registration with Zenodo DOI).

- **Variance decomposition**: translator effect ~20%, source-content effect ~39%, tradition effect ~41% of total within/cross-tradition variance.
- **Translator-as-confound bounded**: pre-registered threshold was 35%; observed 19.5%. Phase 1a's cross-tradition CCB signal is not primarily a translator artifact.
- **Pre-registered hypothesis H1b.3 informatively refuted**: the permutation-test direction in the prereg was backwards; observed shows highly significant translator effect (z ≈ −17.9) in the opposite direction from predicted. Pre-registration discipline surfaces both post-hoc fitting and researcher confusion about the test's own meaning.
- **Calibration error**: pre-registered absolute cosine values were systematically too low across all four pair types; ratios and ordering were correct. Methodology lesson added to §8: report CCB in relative terms, not absolute cosines.

The *between-translator* form of translator-as-confound is bounded. The *within-anglophone-scholar-tradition shared-consensus* form (Katz 1978's broader objection) remains unaddressed and is the explicit target of Phase 1c (multilingual source analysis).

### Phase 2 — multilingual originals (9 languages, LaBSE)

> **⚠ Update (2026-05-24) — robustness correction.** Subsequent multi-model testing (LaBSE vs OpenAI `text-embedding-3-large`) shows the *per-concept cross-lingual dissociation* described below is **model-specific, not robust**: across embedding models the per-concept CCB scores do not agree (cross-model rank correlation ≈ −0.4 in the cross-lingual setting), so "AWARENESS = vocabulary / SUBSTRATE = structural" reflects LaBSE's particular geometry rather than a model-independent fact. The **model-robust** cross-lingual finding is a *holistic concept-geometry isomorphism* measured by Representational Similarity Analysis (RSA), which agrees across models (r ≈ 0.78): the overall arrangement of contemplative concepts is moderately isomorphic across independent linguistic traditions, though the convergence cannot be reliably attributed to any single concept. **The English Phase 0/1 results are unaffected** — CCB and RSA are both model-robust *within* a language (r ≈ 0.88 / 0.97); the fragility is specific to the cross-lingual alignment step. Cross-lingual analysis is migrating to RSA. The per-concept descriptions below are retained for transparency and should be read through this correction. (The full multi-model methods writeup accompanies the forthcoming reframed paper.)
>
> **Further update (2026-07-05, this release) — RSA controls.** Two additional controls discipline the RSA result itself: the instrument is highly reliable (split-half ceiling 0.93–0.95; the isomorphism recovers 42–46% of it), **but the isomorphism does not exceed an arbitrary word-set baseline matched for per-language prevalence** (it falls at the 12th–14th percentile of that baseline on both models). The holistic convergence claim is therefore currently *open, not established*, and the Phase 3a pre-registration adopts the matched word-set baseline as its primary null. Separately, the English Phase 0/1 results survived a lexical-overlap control battery (binding retains 64–90% of its magnitude with the tag terms themselves deleted). See [`findings/phase2c-rsa-controls.md`](findings/phase2c-rsa-controls.md) and [`findings/phase1a-lexical-controls.md`](findings/phase1a-lexical-controls.md).

Phase 1a/1b are English-only. The deepest form of translator-as-confound is that the anglophone scholar-translation tradition could impose convergence invisibly to any *between-translator* test. Phase 2 removes English translation entirely — original-language and same-work parallel texts across nine languages (Chinese, Arabic, Hindi, Hebrew, Spanish, French, Japanese, Greek, English) on the multilingual model **LaBSE** (multilingual-e5 was tested and rejected for CCB — it anisotropically collapses non-English text into a narrow cosine cone). The result reorganizes the project's headline from "five concepts bind" to a **dissociation**:

- **AWARENESS convergence is vocabulary-mediated.** It binds wherever two traditions share an awareness-lexicon (shared lineage, shared school, or translation) and goes **flat** across original-language traditions whose lexicons diverge. The decisive control holds the works fixed and varies only language: the *same two texts* (Dhammapada × Tao Te Ching) bind AWARENESS in English translation but **not** in the original classical Chinese.
- **SUBSTRATE convergence is translation-free.** It binds across original languages with no shared vocabulary and no translation step (cross-language originals +0.0066, *p* = 0.004; within-language originals +0.033, *p* < 0.0001), and survives an operationalization-freeze (a single harmonized cross-language dictionary) and corpus-bulking.
- **ULTIMATE is role/name-bound** — strong within each language, weakest across (God / Allah / Dao / Brahman do not co-locate cross-lingually).

A 40-language **Bible × Quran baseline** supplies per-language reliability and resolution references and an instructive negative result: ULTIMATE convergence is only *middling* between two traditions that worship the **same God**, because God-talk saturates scripture and ubiquity destroys discriminative power. **CCB therefore measures contextual co-location, not shared reference** — which is what licenses reading SUBSTRATE's translation-free binding as structural rather than nominal. A **profile-fit reliability metric** (each language's concept-profile vs. the 40-language consensus on the fixed corpus) gives the first reliability check with a built-in ground truth.

Net (read through the correction above): *on LaBSE* this looks like a clean dissociation of structural from vocabulary-driven convergence — but the per-concept pattern does **not** replicate across embedding models, so it is not a robust finding. The robust cross-lingual result is the holistic RSA concept-geometry isomorphism (model-agreement r ≈ 0.78); the multi-model robustness correction is summarized in the box above. See [`findings/phase2a-summary.md`](findings/phase2a-summary.md) and [`findings/phase2c-originals-only.md`](findings/phase2c-originals-only.md) for the original LaBSE analyses.

### Phase 3a — FORMALLY PRE-REGISTERED BY THIS RELEASE (2026-07-05; not yet run)

The one confound remaining after translation is removed is **genealogical relatedness**: every tradition pair measured in Phases 0–2 shares historical lineage or contact. **Phase 3a** is the project's first *confirmatory* test, designed to isolate it: genealogically independent Axial-Age spheres (pre-Buddhist China × pre-contact Greece, original languages), an age/contact gradient within each sphere, imported Chinese Buddhism as a known-diffusion internal reference, and tiered control concepts (shared-experience anchors vs. functional/convergent-evolution anchors) that replace an abstract zero with real reference points.

**[`findings/phase3a-preregistration.md`](findings/phase3a-preregistration.md) is the frozen registration of record: its publication in this release *is* the act of pre-registration** (timestamp = this public commit + the Zenodo DOI of this release). The registered instrument is holistic RSA concept-geometry isomorphism, the only instrument that survived the multi-model due-diligence (see the correction box above); per-concept claims are pre-declared exploratory; the corpus-sizing rule, adequacy gate, and descriptive fallback are frozen in the registration; no cross-sphere China×Greece value has been computed. The complete design and due-diligence trail is published alongside it: [`findings/phase3a-design.md`](findings/phase3a-design.md), [`findings/phase3a-instrument-tests.md`](findings/phase3a-instrument-tests.md), [`findings/phase3a-rsa-snr-sizing.md`](findings/phase3a-rsa-snr-sizing.md).

The current public paper is **`paper/paper-draft-v8.md` (Draft 8)**: Phase 2 in full, the §6.9 model-robustness correction, the §6.10 RSA result, the Phase 2b same-works mirror gradient, and §8 summarizing the Phase 3a registration. Earlier drafts preserved unchanged: `paper-draft.md` (Draft 2), `paper-draft-v3.md`, `paper-draft-v4.md`, `paper-draft-v5.md`, `paper-draft-v6.md`. `findings/` has per-run write-ups for all phases.

## Quick start

```bash
python -m venv .venv
.venv/Scripts/activate                       # Windows; or source .venv/bin/activate on Unix
pip install -r requirements.txt
echo YOUR_OPENAI_API_KEY > .openai_key       # if using OpenAI backend

# Document-level analysis
python scripts/prototype.py --backend openai --model text-embedding-3-large

# Vocabulary substitution (note: see methodology-notes.md re: shared-placeholder bias)
python scripts/substitute.py
python scripts/prototype.py --corpus corpus/passages_substituted.jsonl \
    --backend openai --model text-embedding-3-large --out results/substituted

# Concept-level analysis (bias-free) — uses unsubstituted embeddings
python scripts/concept_analysis.py

# Sentence-level concept analysis with OpenAI
python scripts/sentence_concept_analysis.py --backend openai

# Sentence-level with local ONNX BERT (no torch — useful on locked-down Windows)
PYTHONPATH=scripts python scripts/sentence_concept_analysis.py --backend onnx \
    --model sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2
```

## What we're trying to produce

1. **A neutral vocabulary** for the structural features the traditions converge on (or, where they don't, a rigorous demonstration that the apparent convergence was hermeneutic projection).
2. **Operationalized claims** — well-posed hypotheses that downstream physics, neuroscience, and philosophy of mind can test independently.
3. **A research API** — a structured translation table connecting traditional vocabularies to neutral structural features, so cross-disciplinary collaboration on these claims becomes legible.
4. **A reusable framework** — the methodology generalizes beyond nondualism; it can be re-run on any claimed convergent concept (Golden Rule, Hero's Journey, eternal recurrence, etc.). See `methodology.md`.

The framework is robust to its own failure: either outcome advances knowledge.

## Status and honest limitations

This is **Phase 0 → Phase 2** — exploratory, not yet rigorously published; Phase 3a (confirmatory) is pre-registered and not yet run. The pipeline-coupling concerns have moved:

- **Translator-as-confound** — now *bounded and partially adjudicated*. Phase 1b bounds the *between-translator* component (~20% of variance); Phase 2 removes English translation entirely and shows the convergence *dissociates* — AWARENESS does not survive translation-removal, SUBSTRATE does. No longer the dominant threat.
- **Genealogical relatedness** — now the largest open confound. Every surviving SUBSTRATE pair shares historical lineage or contact. Phase 3a (a pre-registered independent-traditions test) is built to isolate it.
- **Model dependence — now measured rather than unknown.** The two-model due-diligence (LaBSE vs OpenAI `text-embedding-3-large`) shows per-concept multilingual CCB is model-fragile (conclusions anti-correlate across models) while holistic RSA agrees (r ≈ 0.78) and English monolingual results are robust on both methods. Everything multilingual and per-concept is therefore LaBSE-scoped; a third, open, non-bitext model is still desirable.
- **Concept-mapping / facet coverage.** A concept's dictionary can key on the wrong facet (e.g. Greek SUBSTRATE keyed on ὕλη = *matter* ≠ emptiness), and a corpus may sample a non-convergent facet — so the structural estimate is a lower bound.
- **Regex tagging** (mitigated by a single harmonized cross-language dictionary, not eliminated) and **adversarial passage selection** remain open; held-out human-validated tagging is still future work.
- **Pre-registration** is formalized for Phase 1b (commit-timestamped + Zenodo) and, as of this release, for **Phase 3a** (`findings/phase3a-preregistration.md`); the Phase 0–2 exploratory results are not pre-registered. Phase 3a has not been run: no cross-sphere China×Greece value exists yet.
- The substitution scheme in `findings/phase0-v0.5-substituted.md` has a known placeholder-sharing bias (see `methodology-notes.md`); the concept-binding analyses supersede it. Modern computational sources (Bostrom, Tegmark, Kastrup, Bohm, Rovelli, etc.) remain Phase 0 only; restoring them on verified text is deferred.
